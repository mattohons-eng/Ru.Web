Chinese food is a variety of tasty and unique food some are set and some are spicy but they are all loved by people around the world

Chow Mein

Chow mein noodles, meaning "stir-fried noodles" in Cantonese, originated in ancient China and evolved through the migration of Chinese immigrants to the United States in the 19th century.

Jiàngyóu jīchì

Soy‑sauce chicken wings developed from traditional Chinese soy‑braising methods that became widespread during the Ming and Qing dynasties.

Jiǎozi

Jiǎozi have a 2,000‑year history in China, originating as a medicinal food in the Eastern Han dynasty and evolving into one of the most culturally significant dishes in northern Chinese cuisine.
